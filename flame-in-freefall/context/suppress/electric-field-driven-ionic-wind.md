# Electric field driven ionic wind
Ionic wind is when high-velocity ions move between two electrodes, transferring their energy to the neutral air molecules through collisions that accelerate the gas in the direction of the ions. Ionic wind can be used to create air currents to control smoke movement and accelerate the extinction of certain types of flames.

Rather than using fans, ionic wind is silent and does not rely on moving parts, which could break. Additionally, ionic wind generators can be more lightweight and vastly more energy efficient. Ionic wind is good for controlling fires in micro-gravity environments, as it allows for air movement control without relying on convection currents and gravity.
