# Materials and ignition
Heating solids can cause pyrolysis, releasing gases and vapours. Material selection must account for the atmosphere and installed configuration. Composition, thickness, exposed area and nearby surfaces matter. Electrical faults and overheating remain potential ignition sources.

# Ventilation
Airflow carries heat, oxygen and smoke. In microgravity, ordinary buoyant rise is greatly reduced. Ventilation can supply oxygen to a fire as well as transport smoke to a detector. More airflow is not automatically safer. No universal safe fan speed or oxygen concentration follows from these descriptions. Vehicle-specific emergency procedures cannot be inferred from this page.

# Evidence needed
Material test results, atmosphere conditions, geometry, heat loads and measured airflow are needed for quantitative comparisons.
