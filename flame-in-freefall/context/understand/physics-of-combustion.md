# Physics of combustion
Pyrolysis (Thermal Decomposition): When solid materials like wood, paper, plastics, or textiles are exposed to high heat (without necessarily catching fire), thermal energy breaks down their chemical bonds which releases flammable gases and organic vapours (such as methane, hydrocarbons, or tars) from the surface. This leads to a chain reaction of decomposition which ultimately could lead to extreme fire hazards
